Bio-Inspired Drone Research

Harmony of Nature & Silent Flight

Welcome to Hoot Tech. Inspired by the silent acoustic stealth of nocturnal owls, our project reimagines drone wing aerodynamics. By integrating nature's leading-edge comb serrations and soft trailing-edge fringes, we achieve whisper-quiet flight and gentle, efficient airflow.
